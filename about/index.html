Our story

Digital tools that serve the mission

For more than 7 years, we’ve helped churches and ministries turn real ministry needs into websites, Bible courses, apps and digital platforms people actually use. More than 100 projects later, we still approach the work the same way: understand the need, solve the right problem and stay involved after launch.

We know the world you work in because it is our world too. We understand Sabbath School, evangelistic series, Pathfinders, church boards, volunteers and the realities behind ministry decisions. You don’t have to spend months explaining how your church works, why the mission matters or why certain things cannot be treated like an ordinary business project.

Many of our longest partnerships began with one practical need. We understood the context, did the work well, earned trust, and kept building together.

Meet our team

The people behind DeepVision

Designers, developers and media makers who serve the church.

Every product we build starts with one question: how will this really help people meet God?

Alex is a Ukrainian tech professional and digital ministry leader. He designs large systems and turns them into products that churches and ministries actually use.

Alex works where technology and ministry meet. He sees how dozens of parts connect, designs the architecture of a product, and keeps coming back to one question: how will this really help people?

That question is behind OhMyBible, the learning platforms and the mission projects the team has built. He prefers work that lasts and can grow, so it keeps serving churches long after launch.
