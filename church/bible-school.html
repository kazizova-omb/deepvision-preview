Bible School

Your own online Bible school: our platform and lessons, set up for your church, with follow-up so every interest reaches a person.

Talk with our teamFree Church Digital Checkup

Where this starts

“We get interests after a series and lose them.”

You may recognise one of these:

  • You collect interests after a series and lose them within a month.
  • There is no next step for someone who is not ready to attend.
  • Your lessons exist on paper only.

What this includes

  • Your own Bible school on our platform, in your church name
  • Lessons ready to run, or your own adapted to the platform
  • Enrollment from your website, an ad or an event page
  • Follow-up, so every student reaches a real person

How it runs

Two meetings, then the work

No surprises in the middle of a project: you know what happens next, and who is doing it. The proposal costs you nothing.

  1. 1

    Get to know you

    A first call about your church or ministry, the people you serve and what you already have.

  2. 2

    Free proposal

    At the second meeting we present a proposal with scope, timeline and price. It costs you nothing.

  3. 3

    Build and support

    If it fits, we build it with you, launch it and stay with you after launch.
