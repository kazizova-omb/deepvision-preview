Before you decide

Questions about SEO & Local Visibility

We already have a Google listing. What more is there?

A listing is the start. People also look on Apple Maps and Bing, they read your website, and they decide whether a first visit feels easy. We keep your details the same everywhere, improve the pages people land on, and check every month what is working.

What is included, and what is extra?

Included: a review of your site and local search, your Google profile kept current, ongoing page and content improvements, and a monthly report. (to be confirmed) Paid advertising is separate, with its own budget.

How long until we see results?

Fixes to your Google profile can show within days to a few weeks. Steady growth in search usually takes three to six months.

Is there a minimum term?

We ask for three months to start, because search takes time to respond. After that it runs month to month, and you can stop with a month’s notice.
