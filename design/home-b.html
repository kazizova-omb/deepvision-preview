FAQ

Questions pastors ask us

Ask us your question→

Our budget is limited. Can we start small?

Yes. We can begin with one clear priority, such as improving your website, helping people find your church locally, or promoting an upcoming event. We’ll define the scope and cost before work begins, so your church can plan confidently.

We already have a website. Do we need a new one?

Not necessarily. We’ll review your current website first. If a new one would offer meaningful advantages for your church, we’ll explain what those are and why we recommend replacing it. If improving your existing site is the more effective way to reach your goals, we’ll tell you that too. Both options are possible. Our recommendation depends on what will serve your church best.

We don’t have a communications team. How much work will this require from us?

The beginning will take some focused involvement from your team. We’ll need content, conversations about your goals, and timely feedback to build a strong foundation. Once that’s in place, we can work much more independently and handle the agreed ongoing tasks with minimal demands on your team.

How much does it cost, and are there ongoing fees?

Each service has a different scope. Our service cards show starting prices, and your proposal will explain what’s included. We clearly separate project costs, ongoing support, and expenses such as hosting, software subscriptions, or advertising.

Do you understand Adventist churches and our mission?

Yes. We’re an Adventist team with experience supporting churches and ministries. We understand the importance of your beliefs, local identity, volunteer involvement, and church board decisions. Your church approves the message we help you communicate.

How will we know whether the work is helping?

We’ll agree on clear goals and track relevant results, such as event registrations, inquiries, and Bible study requests. We’ll also provide analytics reports showing what has changed over time, what’s working, and where adjustments are needed. This helps your church see the results and make informed decisions about the next steps.

How long will it take?

We’ll provide a timeline based on your project’s scope. In our experience, the most common delays happen while waiting for content, feedback, or approvals from busy church teams. To keep things moving, we agree on clear responsibilities and response times at the start. With those commitments in place, we can work efficiently and maintain steady progress together.

What happens after launch? Can we manage things ourselves?

We can help your team manage everyday updates or provide ongoing support. Before launch, we’ll clarify access, training, maintenance, and any recurring costs, so you know who is responsible for what.
