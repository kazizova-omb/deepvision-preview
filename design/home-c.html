FAQ

Questions we hear most

Short answers for pastors, boards and ministry leaders.

Who is DeepVision Media, and are you part of the Seventh-day Adventist Church?

DeepVision Media is an independent, Adventist-led team of designers, developers and media makers. We serve Adventist churches, schools and ministries, but we are not an official entity of the Seventh-day Adventist Church, the General Conference or any division or conference.

Who else is part of DeepVision Group?

DeepVision Media is part of DeepVision Group. Our sister agency, DeepVision Marketing, is a YouTube and video marketing agency, so a project that needs channel strategy stays inside one group, with one team and one contract.

Do you work with churches, ministries, or both?

Both, through two starting points. DeepVision for Churches helps local congregations with websites, visibility, social media, prayer room, Sabbath School and church tools. DeepVision Lab builds for ministries, schools and organizations: Bible schools, online courses, web and mobile apps, AI and media.

For churchesFor ministries

How much does it cost to work with you?

It depends on where you start. Church projects start from $1,500, and custom platforms for ministries start from $10,000. Before you commit, we prepare a proposal with the exact scope and price, free of charge.

We already have a website or use a free platform. Can you work with that?

Yes. We build on what you already have: free denominational platforms, an existing website, your volunteer team. Often the best first step is to fix and relaunch what is there rather than start over.

How does a project start?

With two meetings. First, a call to get to know your church or ministry and what you already have. Then we present a free proposal with scope, timeline and price. If it fits, we build and stay with you after launch; if not, you owe nothing.

Do you work outside the United States?

Yes. We work remotely with churches and ministries in the United States, Australia and the United Kingdom.
