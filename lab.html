FAQ

Questions ministry leaders ask us

Ask us your question→

Will you build our course or app turnkey?

Yes. We handle the full cycle: structure and methodology, design, content production, the platform, including our own Clevio, and launch. Then we stay to improve it.

What budget should we expect for a custom platform or app?

Custom platforms and apps typically start at $10,000, and we have delivered projects approaching $1 million. A paid discovery phase gives you a precise scope and estimate before you commit.

We already have a website or course that isn't working. Can you fix it?

Yes. We start by finding out what exactly isn't working, then recommend the smallest change that makes the biggest difference: a redesign, a new structure, or modern features rather than a rebuild.

How do you use AI in ministry projects?

As an enhancer, never a substitute. AI speeds up drafts, translations, tagging and illustration; people review every output, and we set doctrinal guardrails with you before anything is published.

How do payments work?

We work in stages: a deposit to start, then payments tied to milestones you can see.
