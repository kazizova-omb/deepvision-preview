Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Who we are

DeepVision Media is a trade name of DV Digital Media LLC, a limited liability company registered in Ukraine (company code45388363). In this policy "we" and "us" mean that company. We decide how the personal information described here is used, which makes us the data controller.

We are an independent company. We are not an official entity of the Seventh-day Adventist Church.

For anything about your personal information, write to hello@deepvision.media.

The short version

  • We collect what you send us through the forms on this site, and we use it to answer you.
  • Analytics and session recordings are off until you accept them, and you can change your mind at any time.
  • We do not sell personal information, and we do not run advertising on this site.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct or delete it.

What we collect and why

The contact form

Your name, email address, church or organization, your role if you give it, the kind of organization you are writing for, and your message. We use these to reply to you and to prepare a proposal if you ask for one. You also receive one email confirming that your message arrived.

The Church Digital Checkup

Your answers are scored in your own browser. Nothing reaches us unless you ask for the report by email. If you do, we receive your church name, your email address and your results, we send you the report, and our team sees the same summary. We only write to you about your results if you tick the box that asks for it.

Emails and calls

If you email us or speak with us, we keep the correspondence and our notes of what was agreed, so we can follow up and do the work.

Technical information

Like every website, the servers that deliver this one receive your IP address, your browser and device type, the pages you request and the time of each request. We use this to keep the site running and secure. When you send a form, a security check (Cloudflare Turnstile) looks at technical signals from your browser to tell people from automated abuse.

Analytics and session recordings, only if you accept

If you accept analytics in the cookie banner, two tools start working:

  • Google Analytics 4 counts visits and shows us which pages are read, roughly where visitors are (country and city, worked out from the IP address, which Google Analytics does not store), what device they use and how they arrived.
  • Microsoft Clarity records how the site is used: mouse movements, clicks, scrolling and the pages seen. It turns these into heatmaps and replays of individual visits. What you type into our forms is masked and is not recorded.

We use both to find the parts of the site that confuse people and fix them. If you reject analytics, or do nothing, neither tool is loaded. The details and the way to change your choice are on the Cookies page.

What we do not ask for

We do not ask for sensitive information such as health details, financial details or anything about a person's private beliefs. Please do not include it in a message to us, and do not send us personal information about church members.

Our legal bases

Where the law asks for a legal basis, as it does in the United Kingdom and the European Economic Area, ours are:

  • Steps before a contract, and the contract itself: answering your enquiry, preparing a proposal and delivering a project.
  • Your consent: analytics and session recordings, and writing to you about your Checkup results. You can withdraw consent at any time, and it is as easy as giving it.
  • Our legitimate interests: keeping the site secure, stopping abuse of the forms and keeping a record of our correspondence with you.
  • Legal obligations: keeping the accounting and tax records the law requires.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information and we do not give it to anyone for their own marketing. We use a small number of service providers, each for one job:

  • Fly.io hosts the website.
  • Cloudflare provides network services, security and the Turnstile check on our forms.
  • Resend delivers the emails our forms send.
  • Our business email provider stores the messages that reach our inbox.
  • Google (Google Analytics 4), only if you accept analytics.
  • Microsoft (Microsoft Clarity), only if you accept analytics.

Google and Microsoft also use the data their tools collect for their own purposes. In Microsoft's case this includes advertising, as described in the Microsoft Privacy Statement. How Google uses data from sites that use its services is described in Google's own explanation. This is one reason both tools stay off until you accept them.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, or to protect our rights.

Where your information is processed

Our company and team are in Ukraine, and the providers above process data in the United States and other countries. These may have different data protection laws from the country you live in. Where the law requires safeguards for such transfers, we rely on the standard contractual terms our providers offer.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries and Checkup requests: up to 24 months after our last contact with you, unless we start working together.
  • Project and client records: for the length of the project and afterwards for as long as accounting and tax law requires.
  • Email delivery logs: a short period set by our email delivery provider.
  • Google Analytics data: up to 14 months.
  • Microsoft Clarity: session recordings for about 30 days, and combined heatmap data for up to 13 months.
  • Server and security logs: a short period set by our hosting and security providers.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and ask us to stop using it. Write to hello@deepvision.media. We reply within 30 days, we do not charge for it, and we will not treat you differently for asking. We may need to confirm that the request comes from you.

United Kingdom and European Economic Area

You have the rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and data portability, and the right to withdraw consent. You can also complain to your data protection authority. In the United Kingdom this is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Australia

You may ask for access to your personal information and for its correction. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

United States

Depending on your state, you may have the right to know what we hold, to have it corrected or deleted, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell personal information. Because analytics providers may use data for their own purposes, some state laws could treat analytics as sharing. For that reason analytics on this site only runs if you opt in, and a Global Privacy Control signal from your browser is treated as a refusal.

Ukraine

You have the rights set out in the Law of Ukraine "On Personal Data Protection", and you may complain to the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights.

Security

We limit access to personal information to the people on our team who need it, and we use providers that encrypt data in transit. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we will tell you and the relevant authorities where the law requires it.

Children

This website is written for adults who lead or serve churches, ministries and schools. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, tell us and we will delete it.

Other websites

This site links to other websites, including our own products. Each has its own privacy policy, and this one does not cover them.

Changes to this policy

When we change how we handle personal information, we update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
