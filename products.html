Our products

Built for real ministry needs

Explore the products behind each part of the journey.

  • AppLive

    OhMyBible

    An interactive Bible experience in one app: explore Scripture through video, stories, audio and guided prayer.

    • Video Bible and Bible stories that bring Scripture to life
    • Guided prayer and prayer talks
    • Reading plans and daily steps that build a habit, with more to come

    Built by our team for the next generation.

    Visit OhMyBible
  • Platform

    Clevio

    Create a simple course or a full interactive learning ecosystem, all on one platform.

    • Interactive lessons, mentorship and rewards that keep learners going
    • Course and school analytics, progress tracking and certification
    • Your own design and domain, course sites, multilingual support and webinars

    The platform behind our online course and Bible school services.

    Visit Clevio
  • MediaLive

    Bible Stock Club

    Accurate, realistic Bible images for sermons, slides, social media and print.

    • A main library of 3,000+ images of Bible stories and themes
    • A free library of 300+ images to start with
    • Scenes reviewed for accuracy to the biblical text

    Also used across our own design work for churches.

    Visit Bible Stock Club
  • Follow-up

    Bible Course

    A next step for seekers who find your church online.

    Connects with your church website and prayer room.
